Run your association by talking to your AI assistant

IAQ Gest is a free, all-in-one management solution for non-profit associations. Thanks to MCP (Model Context Protocol), every board member connects their own assistant —Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot…— to their account and manages members, fees, accounting or activities just by chatting. And your data stays on your computer.

Designed for AI: MCP architecture

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the open standard AI assistants use to work with other programs' tools. IAQ Gest offers more than 80 tools over MCP, so every board member can connect their own assistant to their account and run the association by chatting.

Each tool has one of three levels —read, write or proposal— and the assistant can only do what its owner's role allows.

👤

Everyone brings their own assistant

Each person connects their AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Cursor, custom agents) to their own account.

🔑

Revocable personal key

Everyone has their own key (revocable, with an expiry date) and the assistant acts with that person's role permissions: the treasurer only sees finances.

🔒

Proposals for important actions

Collecting a direct debit batch, deleting or publishing become proposals that the person approves with one click.

🔍

Never sees sensitive data

ID numbers (DNI/NIE/NIF), IBANs and the like are filtered out automatically before they reach the model.

🔗

Connect Claude.ai and ChatGPT via OAuth

A secure, private connection with a one-time code.

📊

Activity log and remote access

Secure access with Tailscale and a log of everything the agent does.

Connect it in a minute

An MCP server over HTTP for Claude.ai and ChatGPT, a stdio bridge for Claude Desktop or Cursor, and an OpenAPI 3.1 description for any other agent. Example for Claude Desktop or Cursor:

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "iaraque-gestio": {
      "command": "node",
      "args": ["scripts/mcp.ts"],
      "env": { "IARAQUE_URL": "http://127.0.0.1:3000", "IARAQUE_CLAU": "iaq_..." }
    }
  }
}

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes, IAQ Gest is completely free and open source, under the MIT licence.

Do I need to know about AI or computers?

No. It installs like any other program and a wizard guides you at the start. Connecting your AI assistant is optional, and the program explains how to do it step by step.

Can I use it without an AI assistant?

Yes. IAQ Gest is a complete program with its own window; the AI assistant is one more way to use it, not a requirement.

Can the assistant see ID numbers or bank accounts?

No. Sensitive data such as ID numbers (DNI/NIE/NIF) and bank accounts is filtered out automatically to protect privacy.

Which languages does it support?

Catalan, Spanish and English: the program, the installer, the setup wizard and the help manual. Each person picks their language and the program remembers it.

Where is the data stored?

Your data is stored on your own computer and is never sent to the cloud without your explicit consent.

Can I use it from home?

Yes, you can use IAQ Gest from home thanks to Tailscale: each board member connects to the association's computer over a private network, without exposing it to the internet. The program's help explains it step by step.
